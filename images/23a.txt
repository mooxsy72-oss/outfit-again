Traditional Russian royal ensemble consists of a floor-length, A-line velvet caftan-gown with concealed pointed-toe black velvet shoes. The outfit features a dominant deep black and metallic gold color distribution with white pearl accents. The gown provides complete body coverage with a high mandarin collar and voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into rigid, elongated cuffs, leaving the head completely bare without any headdresses, hair ornaments, or pearl strands. It is constructed from heavy, dense black velvet that retains structure and forms deep, vertical drapes. Symmetrical, medium-scale gold embroidery with fleur-de-lis and floral motifs heavily embellishes the collar, front closure, cuffs, and lower skirt.
