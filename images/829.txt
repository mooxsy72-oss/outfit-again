This grunge-inspired casual outfit features a dominant solid black color distribution paired with a secondary white micro-pattern and metallic gold accents.
The upper layer is an oversized, chunky-knit sweater in solid black, crafted from a thick, heavy wool blend with a visible ribbed texture. It features a simple crew neckline, dropped shoulders, and loose, voluminous sleeves that bunch slightly near the ribbed cuffs, ending at the natural waist.
The lower garment is a high-waisted mini-skirt made of lightweight, fluid chiffon or rayon that sits flat at the waist and flares gently into an A-line silhouette ending above the knee. The fabric features a dense, small-scale white ditsy floral print evenly spaced across the black background. On the feet are heavy, black leather combat boots featuring a shiny finish, front laces with silver metal eyelets, and a thick, chunky rubber platform sole with distinctive yellow contrast welt stitching. Accessories include a structured black leather shoulder bag with a short strap and a delicate gold chain necklace with a large, round metallic coin pendant.
