This cottagecore-inspired outfit features a dominant off-white color palette accented by a delicate blue floral print, evoking a soft, romantic aesthetic.
The primary layer is a fitted mini dress crafted from lightweight, fluid cotton fabric. It features a dense, small-scale blue and yellow floral pattern over an off-white background. The dress has a scoop neckline and wide shoulder straps, both trimmed with a prominent ruffled edge that adds soft volume. A matching ruffled trim finishes the straight hem at the mid-thigh.
Layered over the dress is an oversized, chunky-knit cardigan in cream wool, which sits off the right shoulder, exposing the neckline and collarbone. It features a relaxed fit, a V-neckline with large buttons, and long, slouchy sleeves with ribbed cuffs. Thick, off-white ribbed slouch socks are bunched around the calves, paired with low-top retro leather sneakers in a matching cream tone.
