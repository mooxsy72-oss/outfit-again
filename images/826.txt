This contemporary casual outfit features a solid color palette dominated by light blue and black, with no visual patterns.
The inner layer is a form-fitting tank top in solid light blue, made from a smooth, stretchy jersey knit fabric that features a deep scoop neckline and covers the midriff. Layered over it is an oversized, unbuttoned shirt-jacket crafted from thick, structured light blue wool flannel or fleece. It includes a classic pointed collar, dropped shoulders, long loose sleeves with buttoned cuffs, and twin chest patch pockets with gold metallic buttons that match the front closure.
The lower garment consists of high-waisted black shorts tailored from a stiff, mid-weight suiting fabric that retains its structure. They feature vertical pleats, a buttoned waistband, and a short length ending at the upper thigh. On the feet are low-top leather sneakers in a matching two-tone light blue and white color scheme.
