This sophisticated business-casual outfit features an absolute monochromatic solid black color palette with layered textures. The primary top layer is a tight, form-fitting ribbed-knit short-sleeved sweater featuring a high crew neckline, which is neatly tucked into the waistband, fully visible with no outer jackets, blazers, or overlays. The lower body is defined by a high-waisted black wrap mini-skirt with a smooth structure and a subtle asymmetrical curved slit at the left front hem. Semitranslucent sheer black tights cover the elongated legs entirely down to the feet. Completing the look is a pair of chunky, slip-on black leather platform loafers featuring an exaggerated lugged sole and a small metallic triangular logo on the upper strap.
