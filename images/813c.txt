This winter streetwear outfit features a vibrant and streamlined lime-green palette, stripped of its bulky outerwear and heavy footwear. The primary upper garment is a lightweight, tight-fitting lime-green sweater crafted from fine-gauge knit fabric, featuring a high turtleneck collar that snugly covers the throat and long, slim-fit straight sleeves. On the lower body, straight-leg lime-green denim trousers offer a structured, relaxed fit, draping down towards the floor, while the head remains completely bare, with the look styled completely barefoot.
