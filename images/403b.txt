OUTFIT: two-piece avant-garde ensemble features a cream-colored palette with high-contrast texturing between a heavily structured top and a fluid, voluminous skirt. The ultra-cropped, off-the-shoulder bandeau top is covered entirely in dense, three-dimensional fabric rosettes, creating an extremely tactile, chunky texture with considerable thickness and rigid shape retention. It exposes the shoulders, collarbones, upper chest, and full midriff, ending immediately below the bust. The high-waisted mini skirt features a smooth, fitted yoke across the hips before erupting into an asymmetric waterfall silhouette. The left side and rear feature tiered layers of sheer, lightweight organza ruffles that cascade dramatically down past the knee, pooling towards the ankle. This sheer fabric is lightweight and floaty, creating soft, fluid visual volume. The right side is aggressively short, exposing the entire right thigh and leg, with the look styled completely barefoot. The outfit is dominated by the asymmetry and sheer cascading volume of the skirt's dramatic ruffle train.
