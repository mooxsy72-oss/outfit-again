This fantasy Slavic folk-inspired ensemble features a dominant steel-blue color palette with secondary ivory-white layers and ornate bronze-gold accents. Providing full body coverage, the under-layer is a floor-length ivory-white linen rubakha shirt decorated with subtle gold floral patterns, featuring long, voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into tight, patterned wrist cuffs. Layered over it is a traditional ankle-length sarafan dress made of mid-weight, structured steel-blue linen with wide shoulder straps and a square neckline. The sarafan features an open center-front slit and a wide border trim heavily embellished with gold geometric embroidery running down the center, torso, and lower hemline. A thick, woven gold-and-blue fabric sash belt tightly pinches the waist, tied with long dangling metallic cords adorned with prominent bronze teardrop tassels hanging down the front. Layered bronze chain necklaces and small teardrop earrings are included, with the look styled completely barefoot beneath the ankle-length hemline.
