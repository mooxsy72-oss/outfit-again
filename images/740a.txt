This casual grunge streetwear outfit features a white and light-blue denim color palette with a relaxed silhouette. The primary top layer is a tight white cropped tank top made of lightweight jersey fabric that leaves the shoulders, collarbones, and a strip of the midriff exposed. The dominant lower layer consists of high-waisted, baggy wide-leg light-wash blue jeans with prominent knee rips and a structured, pooling floor-length drape. On the feet are simple black and white low-top canvas sneakers with flat white rubber soles.
