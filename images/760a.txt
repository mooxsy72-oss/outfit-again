This cozy, preppy autumn outfit features an oversized brown sweater, a white t-shirt, a plaid mini skirt, and Mary Jane shoes. The heavy-knit brown crewneck sweater features a loose, oversized silhouette with drop shoulders and long sleeves ending in ribbed cuffs. Layered beneath is a fitted, lightweight white crewneck t-shirt. The high-waisted A-line mini skirt is crafted from thick, structured wool-blend fabric with a dense plaid pattern in shades of brown, beige, and black. Glossy brown patent leather Mary Jane shoes feature a low block heel and two parallel instep straps with silver buckles.
