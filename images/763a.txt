This Slavic folk-inspired ensemble features a dominant dark brown and cream palette with a secondary forest-green hemline accent and colorful embroidery, providing full body coverage. The under-layer is a loose, lightweight cream-white linen shirt or sorochka featuring a high mock collar and voluminous, billowy bishop sleeves gathered at the wrists. Layered over it is a fluidly draped, floor-length dark brown cotton sarafan dress with a gathered waistline. The dress features a high-necked, rectangular chest panel heavily adorned with red, teal, and orange floral folk embroidery, framed by short, ruffled flutter sleeves. The lower third of the full, flowing skirt shifts into a wide forest-green panel decorated with a repeating pattern of red flowers and a geometric embroidered border trim at the floor-length hem, leaving the head completely bare without any headbands or hair ornaments. Footwear consists of antique-style, light brown leather lace-up ankle boots featuring pointed toes and supportive block heels.
