This modern equestrian-inspired outfit features a coordinated brown and beige houndstooth pattern as the dominant visual element, paired with solid dark brown secondary layers and gold accents. The outerwear is a thick, structured cropped wool jacket with long wide sleeves, a standing band collar, and an intentionally boxy silhouette that exposes the lower waist. It layers over a fitted, lightweight dark brown sleeveless mock-neck top that tucks into matching high-waisted houndstooth shorts, leaving the lower thighs partially exposed. Footwear consists of prominent, knee-high dark brown suede boots featuring a sharply pointed toe and a slender stiletto heel. Accessories include small gold hoop earrings and an oval gold wristwatch with a brown strap.
