This modern streetwear autumn outfit features a balanced palette dominated by solid black and heather grey, accented by secondary olive green. Providing full body coverage, the inner top layer is a loose-fitting, light heather grey cotton fleece hoodie with long sleeves and a relaxed drawstring hood. Layered over it is a slightly oversized, cropped olive-green jacket crafted from thick, heavily textured faux-shearling teddy fleece, featuring a pointed shirt collar, drop shoulders, and dual buttoned chest pockets. The lower garment consists of loose, relaxed-fit matte black sweatpants with a gathered elastic waistband and elasticated cuffs. Footwear consists of crisp white low-top athletic sneakers featuring flat rubber soles. A structured black leather camera-style crossbody bag hangs across the chest.
