This edgy winter streetwear outfit features a dominant monochromatic black leather texture. The primary upper garment is a sleek, tight-fitting black knit sweater with long fitted sleeves that closely hugs the torso, leaving a small strip of the midriff exposed. The matching lower layer consists of high-waisted, wide-leg black faux-leather trousers that provide a heavy, structured drape pooling slightly at the floor. Completing the look is a pair of rugged black leather combat boots featuring thick, lugged platform soles, block heels, and front laces.
