This contemporary casual outfit features a vibrant royal blue and white color palette. The primary upper garment is a tight-fitting white t-shirt-style top crafted from smooth cotton, featuring short fitted sleeves and a deeply scooped low-cut neckline that opens the chest and collarbones, completely free of any outer jackets, shackets, or overlays. The top is cleanly tucked into the high waistband of a pair of tailored shorts in an intense royal blue hue, leaving the legs completely exposed. The black leather sandals feature thin stiletto heels and delicate criss-cross straps that wrap around the instep and tie above the ankles. Multiple stacked chunky gold bracelets on both wrists and large gold hoop earrings complete the look.
