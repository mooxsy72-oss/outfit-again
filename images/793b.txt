This modern evening outfit features a vibrant black and leopard-print color palette, stripped of its footwear and hosiery accessories. The fluid blouse dominates the upper silhouette with extreme, billowy balloon sleeves gathered into tight cuffs, exposing the right collarbone, shoulder, and neckline. The structured mini skirt, made of mid-weight jacquard or heavyweight cotton with a dense all-over brown and black leopard pattern, creates a distinct A-line shape. The neck, chest, both wrists, and the entire legs are left completely bare, with the look styled completely barefoot.
