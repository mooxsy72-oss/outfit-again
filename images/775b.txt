This historical Slavic folk-inspired ensemble features a dominant ivory-beige and dark brown color palette accented with red geometric embroidery. The primary upper garment is a loose, standard-length linen rubakha shirt that rests just down to the upper hips, fully visible with its torso left completely un-cinched and free of any leather vests or cords. Red geometric cross-stitch folk embroidery frames the neckline, sleeve bicep bands, cuffs, and lower hemline. Beneath the shirt are loose dark trousers, with the look styled completely barefoot, free of any boots or footwear.
