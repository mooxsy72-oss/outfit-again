This academic-inspired preppy outfit features a structured silhouette dominated by black, balanced by a secondary deep teal blue plaid pattern.
The top layer is a cropped, V-neck cardigan knitted from a thick, fuzzy mohair-blend yarn. It features a muted teal base with a dark grey plaid grid pattern, framed by solid black ribbed knit edges along the placket, hem, and cuffs. The cardigan is secured with three large, glossy black decorative buttons, leaving a small gap of the midriff exposed. Beneath it, a solid black cropped camisole covers the chest.
The lower half consists of a high-waisted, structured black mini-skirt with sharp, dense accordion pleats that flare out slightly at the mid-thigh. The legs are covered in sheer black tights. Accessories include a black quilted leather bucket bag with a gold chain strap worn over the shoulder, and black leather Mary Jane flats with thin instep straps.
