This fantasy Slavic folk-inspired ensemble features a dominant steel-blue color palette with secondary ivory-white layers and ornate bronze-gold accents. Providing full body coverage, the under-layer is a floor-length ivory-white linen rubakha shirt decorated with subtle gold floral patterns, featuring long, voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into tight, patterned wrist cuffs. Layered over it is a traditional ankle-length sarafan dress made of mid-weight, structured steel-blue linen with wide shoulder straps and a square neckline. The sarafan features an open center-front slit and a wide border trim heavily embellished with gold geometric embroidery running down the center, torso, and lower hemline. A thick, woven gold-and-blue fabric sash belt tightly pinches the waist, tied with long dangling metallic cords adorned with prominent bronze teardrop tassels hanging down the front. Footwear consists of antique-style brown leather shoes with center-stitched seams and flat soles, paired with layered bronze chain necklaces and small teardrop earrings.
