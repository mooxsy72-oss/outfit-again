This casual athleisure outfit features a coordinated monochromatic green color palette paired with cream accents. The primary top layer is an oversized olive-green ribbed-knit sweater featuring a high half-zip collar, dropped shoulders, and loose sleeves with tight ribbed cuffs. Layered over it is a secondary, bulky olive-green puffer vest with horizontal quilting and a wide, open stand-up collar adding visual volume. The lower body consists of skin-tight forest-green ribbed leggings that fully cover the legs. Accessories include olive-green over-ear headphones worn on the head and thick, bunched-up cream ribbed socks at the ankles. Completing the look is a pair of chunky, flat cream suede Ugg-style boots with soft shearling details.
