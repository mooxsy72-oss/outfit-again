This modern casual streetwear outfit features a balanced black and pastel pink color palette. The dominant upper layer is a bulky, cropped black puffer jacket made of structured, horizontally quilted synthetic fabric. It features a high stand-up collar, padded drop-shoulder panels, and long full sleeves with elasticated cuffs. The right chest displays pink embroidered "Hello Kitty" cursive script, while the left chest features a prominent white Hello Kitty face patch with a pink bow. On the lower body, straight-leg light pink denim jeans provide a stiff, relaxed fit down to the ankles. Completing the look is a solid pastel pink ribbed-knit beanie hat and matching pink and black mid-top athletic sneakers with structured panels and laces.
