This modern casual summer outfit features a monochromatic solid black color palette. The single dominating garment is a structured short-sleeved romper jumpsuit made from a mid-weight, crisp cotton poplin fabric with strong shape retention. It features a tailored silhouette with a notched lapel collar, a front button-down closure using small brown contrasting buttons, and short sleeves with rolled-up cuffs. The waist is tightly defined by a matching fabric belt tied in a large bow at the front. The relaxed-fit, slightly flared shorts section includes side slit pockets and exposes the full length of the legs. On the feet are secondary black leather double-strap slide sandals featuring chunky molded soles and prominent rectangular gold buckles.
