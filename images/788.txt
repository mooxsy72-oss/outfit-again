This modern smart-casual classic menswear ensemble relies on a balanced palette of solid charcoal grey and rich tawny brown, providing total body coverage. The upper garment is a skin-tight, long-sleeved turtleneck sweater crafted from lightweight, tightly knit wool with a fine, smooth surface texture. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, tailored trousers made of thick, heavily textured donegal wool tweed featuring a multicolored flecked pattern in orange, gold, and brown. They feature prominent double front pleats, sharp pressed center creases, and a wide waistband with buttoned side-adjuster tabs. Footwear consists of matching dark brown leather oxford dress shoes featuring low stacked heels and almond toes.
