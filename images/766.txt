This traditional Ukrainian folk-inspired ensemble features a dominant crisp white and deep red color palette adorned with vibrant floral and geometric patterns. Providing full body coverage, the upper garment is an authentic linen blouse (vyshyvanka) featuring a high mandarin collar and straight, loose sleeves. The collar, shoulders, central chest bib, and wide cuffs are densely detailed with multi-colored floral embroidery and geometric cross-stitch bands in red, black, and blue. The lower garment is a heavy, floor-length, deep red wool-blend plakhta-style skirt that gathers neatly into a wide, matching deep red fabric sash belt (poyas), wrapped around the waist and knotted on the left hip with two hanging tasseled cords. Concealed entirely beneath the sweeping hemline are matching dark red leather ankle boots with flat soles.
