This Slavic and Central Asian folk-inspired costume features a brick-red and maroon palette with cream and gold accents. The upper garment is a structured, high-collared bodice with a mandarin collar, straight sleeves, and tight cuffs. The collar, shoulders, and cuffs are covered in dense red and gold geometric folk embroidery. The sleeves feature a faded maroon paisley print. The lower garment is a full-length, heavily gathered red skirt adorned with a faded cream and gold botanical pattern down to the hemline. A narrow woven textile sash wraps the waist, knotted with long fringed tassels and dangling dark brown feathers.
