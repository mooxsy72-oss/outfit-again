This modern cozy autumn athleisure look features a dominant palette of warm tan, khaki-brown, and taupe tones, accented by a solid cream base layer. The base layer is a skin-tight, cropped white sports camisole with a square scoop neckline and thin elastic straps. Layered over it is a cropped, loose-fitting taupe sweater in thick, ribbed knit wool with long billowy sleeves, wide ribbed cuffs, a ribbed hemline, and a wide collar framing a short quarter-zip metallic closure, fully visible with no outer puffer jackets or heavy outerwear layers. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, form-fitting khaki-brown leggings crafted from a smooth, highly elastic compressive performance fabric. Layered over the ankles are thick, slouched cream knit crew socks with a ribbed texture. Footwear consists of tan suede low-profile slip-on slippers featuring flat rubber soles, fuzzy cream shearling interior linings, and a decorative white-and-brown braided fabric trim running along the openings.
