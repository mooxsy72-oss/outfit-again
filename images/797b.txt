This elegant autumn outfit features a gentle cream, light blue, and deep brown color palette, stripped of its heavy outerwear layer. The primary upper garment is the cropped white ribbed-knit sweater featuring an exaggerated high turtleneck and long, dramatic bell sleeves that flare out widely at the cuffs, creating a striking and cozy focal point with no outer coats or jackets. The lower body is defined by high-waisted, secondary light-wash blue denim jeans with a structured, wide-leg silhouette that drapes to the floor. On the feet are dark brown leather ankle boots with chunky mid-heels and rounded toes.
