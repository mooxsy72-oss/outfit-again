This modern preppy outfit features a monochromatic deep burgundy color palette with a crisp white accent. The dominant layer is an oversized burgundy cable-knit sweater with slouchy balloon sleeves and a ribbed crew neckline. Layered underneath, a white cotton shirt peeks out, showing its structured pointed collar and clean white cuffs. The sweater is casually tucked into a high-waisted burgundy leather mini-skirt that provides a smooth, rigid contrast to the knit texture. Covering the legs are secondary semi-opaque burgundy tights that fully enclose the lower body down to the feet. Completing the look are matching burgundy patent leather stiletto pumps with sharply pointed toes and thin ankle straps.
