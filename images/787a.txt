This modern minimalist streetwear outfit features a monochrome color scheme dominated by solid, unpatterned panels of off-white and charcoal black, providing near-total body coverage. The upper garment is a relaxed, intentionally oversized crewneck sweater crafted from a thick, mid-weight off-white knit fabric with a subtle vertical ribbed texture. It features a simple round crew neckline, heavily dropped shoulders, and loose, long sleeves draping past the wrists. A straight, clean white undershirt hemline peeks out slightly at the waist. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, extremely loose and fluid charcoal black wide-leg trousers made from a lightweight, flowing suiting fabric that drapes heavily down to the floor, with the look styled completely barefoot.
