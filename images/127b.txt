This shamanic fantasy ensemble is crafted from an oatmeal-colored, mid-weight woven linen with a slubby texture, stripped of its heavy outer layers. The primary garment is now a simple, straight-cut, mid-calf tunic with narrow sleeves, completely exposing the neck, head, and bare arms, while leaving the feet completely barefoot. The chest features layered necklaces combining multi-strand wooden beads, small skulls, and a central long leg bone pendant.
