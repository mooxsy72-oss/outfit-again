This historical Early Medieval Slavic-inspired ensemble features a natural grey-beige dominant color with secondary dark brown and deep red accents. The core garment is a loose, straight-cut, floor-length rubakha tunic gown crafted from textured, coarse linen fabric. It features a split V-neckline, long wide sleeves, and a straight hemline, all meticulously bordered by dense red geometric cross-stitch folk embroidery. Layered on the left hip is a long, rectangular dark brown linen apron panel showcasing an embroidered red geometric border and a raw fringed edge. A narrow, multi-strand twisted brown fabric sash tightly cinches the waist, tied with long tasseled cords. The look is completed by antique-style dark brown leather boots featuring flat soles and rounded toes, rising mid-calf beneath the straight hemline.
