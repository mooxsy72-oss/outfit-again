This casual everyday outfit pairs a black knit sweater with a high-waisted denim maxi skirt, a matching leather belt, and flat slingback shoes. The relaxed black sweater, made from a mid-weight ribbed knit, features a classic round crew neck, dropped shoulders, and loose, long sleeves ending in fitted ribbed cuffs. It is tucked into a structured medium-wash blue denim maxi skirt. The rigid, high-waisted skirt features vertical seams, a high-rise waist with belt loops, and a prominent center-front slit extending from the lower thigh to the straight ankle-length hem. A thick black leather belt with a large, polished gold-tone circular buckle cinches the waist. Flat black slingback shoes featuring a glossy patent leather pointed toe and a thin adjustable ankle strap complete the look.
