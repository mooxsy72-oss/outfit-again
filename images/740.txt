This casual grunge streetwear outfit features a dark plaid, white, and light-blue denim color palette with an oversized, relaxed silhouette. The outer layer is an oversized dark grey and brown plaid flannel shirt with long loose sleeves, dropped shoulders draped off one side, and a pointed collar left unbuttoned. Underneath, a tight white cropped tank top made of lightweight jersey fabric leaves the collarbones, left shoulder, and a strip of the midriff exposed. The dominant lower layer consists of high-waisted, baggy wide-leg light-wash blue jeans with prominent knee rips and a structured, pooling floor-length drape. On the feet are simple black and white low-top canvas sneakers with flat white rubber soles.
