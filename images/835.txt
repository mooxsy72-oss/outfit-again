This nautical-inspired preppy casual outfit features a balanced neutral color distribution dominated by solid black and a cream-and-navy striped pattern, accented by metallic gold details.
The upper garment is a relaxed-fit knit polo shirt crafted from medium-weight, textured open-stitch cotton crochet. It features a cream base with thin, evenly spaced horizontal navy-blue stripes. The top includes a flat cuban collar with an unbuttoned, open V-neckline, short straight sleeves, and a ribbed hem ending at the natural waist. The lower garment is a high-waisted mini-skirt tailored from a thick, structured black wool-blend suiting fabric. It features a straight A-line silhouette, full coverage through the hips, and a small vertical slit on the left thigh hem.
On the feet are structured black patent-leather penny loafers with low block heels and gold metallic logo coins inserted into the front straps. Wearable accessories include thick gold hoop earrings with engraved branding, tortoishell-patterned cat-eye sunglasses with gold temples, and twin beige hair clips.
