This glamorous winter outfit features an absolute monochromatic solid black color palette with contrasting textures. The dominant upper garment is a voluminous, cropped black faux-fur jacket made from plush, thick material, featuring a wide shawl collar, open front, and loose, slightly flared full sleeves that add substantial visual volume to the upper silhouette. The matching lower layer consists of high-waisted, wide-leg black leather trousers providing a heavy, rigid drape that pools elegantly over the footwear at the floor. A classic black suede tote handbag with structured top handles is included. Completing the ensemble is a pair of sleek black leather ankle boots featuring sharply pointed toes, thick high block heels, and a thin ankle strap secured with a small round gold buckle.
