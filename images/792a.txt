This look features an all-black, contemporary academia aesthetic combining tailored and cropped elements. The dominant piece is an oversized, structured, double-breasted blazer with sharp shoulders, wide notch lapels, long sleeves, and a long straight hem that hangs low over a matching slim-fit, high-waisted mini skirt with a subtle front side slit. Peeking from underneath is a tight, cropped mock-neck top that leaves the midriff exposed. The legs are partially covered by sheer, knee-high black socks. On the feet are chunky, thick-soled leather loafers featuring a distinctive silver triangular emblem on the tongue, leaving the hands and shoulders completely free of any satchel bags or straps. Silver heart-shaped drop earrings complete the ensemble.
