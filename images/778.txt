This modern lounge sportswear outfit features a monochrome color scheme dominated by shades of off-white and light heather grey. The inner layer is a skin-tight, cropped tank top in off-white ribbed cotton knit, featuring a deep scoop henley neckline with a functional, unbuttoned three-button placket. The midriff, navel, and right shoulder are exposed. Layered over it is a lightweight, loose-fitting off-white ribbed knit cardigan shrug, worn slouchy and completely off the right shoulder, featuring long straight sleeves with ribbed cuffs. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, baggy light heather grey sweatpants crafted from thick fleece fabric, featuring a gathered elastic waistband and a long, functional drawstring tie at the center front. The look finishes with matching pristine white low-top athletic sneakers featuring flat rubber soles.
