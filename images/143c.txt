This traditional Russian folk costume offers complete coverage, pairing a white rubakha (chemise) with a dominant burgundy sarafan (pinafore dress). The mid-weight rubakha features a gathered neckline and voluminous, billowy balloon sleeves tapering into tight cuffs. Layered over it, the floor-length, heavy-weight sarafan creates a stiff A-line drape, decorated with a vertical gold-embroidered geometric trim down the front center and a matching thick band along the wide hem, leaving the head completely bare without any headdresses or hair ornaments, with the look styled completely barefoot.
