This traditional Russian folk ensemble features an ankle-length outfit, stripped of its front overlays. The head remains completely bare without any headdresses. The core garment is a structured, heavy sarafan dress rendered in a dark gold and black floral-patterned brocade, featuring a high-waisted bodice and a wide, flowing skirt, fully exposing the front panel of the brocade dress without any aprons, fabric sashes, or waist ties. Layered underneath is the voluminous white linen rubakha shirt with oversized, billowy bishop sleeves. On the feet are woven bast lapti shoes paired with thick white linen leg wraps cross-tied up to the shins.
