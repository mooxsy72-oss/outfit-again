This modern casual lounge set features a strict monochrome color scheme dominated entirely by a uniform heather grey palette. The outfit consists of a two-piece ribbed knit ensemble with an all-over vertical rib pattern. The top is a tight-fitting, long-sleeved shirt featuring an asymmetrical boat neckline worn completely off the right shoulder, long fitted sleeves, and an overlapping, angled handkerchief hemline that slants across the upper hips. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, loose wide-leg trousers that drape fluidly over the lower body and pool softly at the ankles, with the look styled completely barefoot. This combination leaves only the right shoulder completely exposed.
