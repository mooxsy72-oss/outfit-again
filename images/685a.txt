This elegant evening look features a dark wine-red mesh maxi dress. The ankle-length gown is constructed from a lightweight, sheer mesh fabric layered over a solid lining, creating an elongated, body-hugging silhouette. The bodice features a square neckline, dense horizontal ruching across the chest and torso, and long, semi-sheer sleeves with slight volume at the shoulders that taper down to the wrists. At the left hip, the fabric gathers tightly into a decorative cascading ruffle trim, which frames a high, dramatic thigh slit that exposes the left leg, with the look styled completely barefoot. The entire look relies on a solid, unpatterned monochromatic color palette.
