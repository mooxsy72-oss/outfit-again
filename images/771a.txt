This fantasy Early Medieval Slavic-inspired costume features an ivory-white dominant color scheme with secondary terracotta-red and bronze accents. Providing full body coverage, the top layer is a calf-length rubakha tunic gown made of textured, mid-weight ivory linen with high side slits and long, wide bell sleeves. The modest round neckline, bicep lines, and wide cuffs are framed by geometric terracotta-red folk embroidery. Layered beneath is a floor-length, deep red linen under-skirt bordered by a metallic gold trim band. A wide, patterned red and gold woven fabric poyas sash wraps the waist, knotted with long cords decorated with prominent bronze bell tassels dangling down the front. Layered bronze chain necklaces complete the ensemble, with the look styled completely barefoot beneath the floor-length under-skirt.
