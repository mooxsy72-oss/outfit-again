This casual summer streetwear outfit features a balanced olive green and white color palette. The outer layer is an oversized, crisp white cotton shirt with a relaxed silhouette, dropped shoulders, and loose full sleeves worn unbuttoned and draped off the shoulders. Underneath, a form-fitting olive green camisole bodysuit features a square neckline and ultra-thin spaghetti straps, leaving the chest and collarbones exposed. On the lower body, high-waisted olive green tailored shorts made from a structured, mid-weight fabric feature front pleats and side pockets, exposing the full length of the legs. The ensemble features no jewelry or wearable accessories. Completing the look is a pair of classic low-top white leather sneakers with matching white laces and thick flat soles.
