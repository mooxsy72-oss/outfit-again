This modern edgy outfit balances a dominant black color scheme with a secondary crisp white layer. It centers on an oversized, mid-thigh length white poplin shirt dress featuring a classic pointed collar, plunging unbuttoned V-neckline, and long sleeves with wide white cuffs peeking through. Layered over the torso is a highly structured, rigid matte black leather underbust corset featuring vertical boning and a pointed base. Atop this sits an ultra-cropped, structured black leather jacket with sharp notched lapels and padded shoulders. The outfit leaves the lower thighs and entire legs exposed, finishing with mid-calf black leather combat boots with thick platform lug soles and front laces. Wearable accessories include a thick black velvet choker holding a large silver heart pendant.
