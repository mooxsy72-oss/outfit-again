This modern casual outfit features a cropped, voluminous quilted jacket in a light beige hue as its primary statement piece. The jacket has an oversized fit with wide, dropped-shoulder balloon sleeves ending in thick ribbed cuffs, a high ribbed collar, and a gathered elastic hem. Beneath it, a tight-fitting, lightweight white crewneck t-shirt provides a smooth layer covering the torso. Highly structured, opaque matte black ankle-length leggings fit like a second skin from the high waist down to the ankles, contrasting with the jacket's bulk. White athletic crew socks are pulled up over the lower leggings. Low-top white leather sneakers with matching laces and a chunky sole anchor the look.
