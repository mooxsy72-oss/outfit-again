This traditional East Slavic ensemble shifts to a indoor or mid-season look, stripped of its heavy winter outerwear. The primary garment is now an authentic, floor-length traditional Slavic dress-sarafan. It consists of a loose-fitting white linen under-shirt featuring long, loose straight sleeves that end in neat cuffs adorned with thin terracotta-red geometric borders. Layered over it is a structured, deep terracotta-red wool-blend pinafore dress with a classic straight and non-contoured historical silhouette that drapes softly down to the floor without emphasizing the waist. The bodice features an intricate central chest panel heavily detailed with multi-colored traditional floral embroidery, while a matching wide geometric border frames the floor-length hem. A wide, patterned red and white woven fabric sash belt wraps tightly around the waist, knotted with long tassels dangling down the front. The head, neck, and hands remain completely bare, and on the feet are the mid-calf light brown leather boots with flat soles.
