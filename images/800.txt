This edgy rocker outfit features a monochromatic solid black color palette. The dominant upper garment is a structured black leather biker jacket with silver metal zippers, a wide asymmetrical lapel collar with metallic snaps, and straight full-length sleeves. Underneath, a tight black knit turtleneck sweater covers the torso up to the throat. The secondary lower layer consists of high-waisted black denim shorts with rolled-up cuffs, secured by a thick black leather belt with a silver grommet pattern and a rectangular buckle. Transparent sheer black tights fully cover the legs down to the feet. Completing the look is a pair of rugged black leather combat boots with thick, lugged platform soles and front laces.
