This glamorous winter outfit features an absolute monochromatic solid black color palette with contrasting textures. The primary upper garment is a sleek, tight-fitting black knit sweater with long fitted sleeves that closely hugs the torso, creating a streamlined upper silhouette. The matching lower layer consists of high-waisted, wide-leg black leather trousers providing a heavy, rigid drape that pools elegantly over the footwear at the floor. Completing the ensemble is a pair of sleek black leather ankle boots featuring sharply pointed toes, thick high block heels, and a thin ankle strap secured with a small round gold buckle.
