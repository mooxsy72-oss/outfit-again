This modern athletic-chic outfit features an oversized cream-white maxi coat layered over a tight, high-contrast base. The heavy wool-blend coat dominates the silhouette with its dramatic, padded, boxy shoulders, wide notched lapels, and structured ankle-length drape. Underneath, a sleek, skin-tight off-white mock-neck top with long sleeves is tucked into opaque, matte black high-waisted leggings that extend down to the ankles. Complete coverage is maintained. White crew socks with black branding elements are pulled over the leggings, flowing into multi-textured white and grey athletic sneakers. A casual black baseball cap with white embroidered lettering finishes the street-style aesthetic.
