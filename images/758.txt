This modern minimalist outfit features a dominant black color palette with a stark white secondary layer and gold accents. An oversized, thigh-length black leather blazer defines the outer silhouette with structured shoulders, wide notch lapels, and functional flap pockets. It layers over a lightweight, tight-fitting white cotton crewneck t-shirt with short sleeves that neatly tucks into a high-waisted black leather A-line mini skirt, leaving the arms and lower thighs exposed. Footwear consists of glossy, crinkled patent leather ankle boots with a square toe and a thick block heel. Accessories include a structured black leather hobo bag with a gold interlocking logo, thick gold hoop earrings, a delicate gold pendant necklace, and a rectangular gold link watch.
