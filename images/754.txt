This modern, sophisticated outfit showcases a dominant solid black color palette contrasted by a secondary taupe-brown layer with gold and green accents. The key outerwear piece is a structured, waist-length taupe-brown tweed jacket featuring a collarless round neckline, long straight sleeves, and four symmetrical front flap pockets decorated with metallic gold buttons. It pairs with high-waisted, relaxed-fit black tailored shorts featuring neat front pleats. Sheer, lightweight black polka-dot tights provide leg coverage down to a pair of structural, knee-high black leather riding boots with a flat heel and a gold logo buckle. Accessories include a structured, woven black leather tote bag with top handles, green gemstone drop earrings, and a rectangular gold link watch.
