This modern, elegant outfit is built around a dominant olive-green base with secondary dark brown tones and prominent gold accents. The central piece is an olive-green, mid-thigh cotton canvas mini dress with an A-line silhouette. Its design features an empire waist, a deep plunging sweetheart neckline, and voluminous, exaggerated puff sleeves gathered with elasticated cuffs that expose the forearms and collarbones. Sheer black tights offer lightweight leg coverage. Footwear consists of dark brown suede slingback pumps with a pointed toe and a slender stiletto heel. Accessories include chunky gold knot stud earrings and a heavy gold chain necklace with a large interlocking-logo pendant.
