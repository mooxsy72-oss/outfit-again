This edgy autumn biker outfit features a dominant monochromatic solid black color palette accented with delicate gold jewelry and no handheld bags. The primary top layer is a loose-fitting, black leather biker jacket with metallic silver hardware, an asymmetrical zipper closure, a wide lapel collar with snaps, and a dangling waist belt with a metal buckle. Underneath sits a baggy, loose black knit turtleneck sweater with dropped shoulders and relaxed sleeves. The matching lower layer consists of high-waisted, form-fitting black leggings in a stretchy, matte knit fabric that fully covers the legs down to the ankles. Fine accessories include a delicate gold chain necklace with a clover pendant and a minimalist gold nail-shaped bangle bracelet. Completing the look is a pair of rugged black leather combat boots featuring thick, lugged platform soles and front laces.
