This fantasy Russian boyar-inspired ensemble features a minimalist cream-white palette, completely stripped of its heavy outer layers and heavy shoulder jewelry. It features a soft fluid drape, and voluminous, heavily gathered long sleeves that taper neatly into tight cuffs at the wrists, while the head remains completely bare, with the look styled completely barefoot.
