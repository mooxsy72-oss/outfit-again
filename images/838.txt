This casual West Coast streetwear outfit features a vibrant contrast color palette dominated by a solid red top, orange footwear, and off-white base accents, excluding any handbag.
The centerpiece is an exaggeratedly oversized red jersey polo-style shirt made of heavy-weight, structured cotton. It features a contrasting off-white pointed collar, a relaxed open placket, and mid-length wide sleeves accented with double off-white athletic stripes. White script embroidery reading "California" spans the chest. Underneath is a fitted red crewneck layer. The lower body is defined by slim-fitting, ankle-length off-white ribbed leggings that contrast with the voluminous top.
On the feet are high-top orange and white leather sneakers with a thick flat sole. Accessories include a light cream bandana with a subtle paisley print tied over the head and two delicate, layered gold chain necklaces.
