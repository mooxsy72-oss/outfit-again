This casual, modern streetwear outfit features a monochromatic black base contrasted with classic blue denim and crisp white elements. The primary layering piece is an ultra-cropped blue denim jacket with a loose, relaxed fit, dropped shoulders, long straight sleeves, a sharp pointed collar, and a heavily frayed, raw-cut cropped hemline. Underneath, a tight black cotton cropped t-shirt features a high crew neckline and short sleeves, leaving a strip of the midriff exposed. The dominant lower garment is a high-waisted, midi-length black ribbed-knit skirt that tightly hugs the hips and thighs, featuring a dramatic, high thigh-slit on the left side that exposes the leg. The look features no jewelry or wearable accessories. Completing the ensemble is a pair of clean low-top white leather athletic sneakers with thick, flat white rubber soles and matching white laces.
