This modern cozy autumn athleisure look features a dominant palette of khaki-brown and tan tones, accented by a solid cream base layer. The primary upper garment is the skin-tight, cropped white sports camisole with a square scoop neckline and thin elastic straps, smoothly hugging the torso and completely exposing the shoulders, arms, and midriff area with no outer sweaters or cardigans. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, form-fitting khaki-brown leggings crafted from a smooth, highly elastic compressive performance fabric. Layered over the ankles are thick, slouched cream knit crew socks with a ribbed texture. Footwear consists of tan suede low-profile slip-on slippers featuring flat rubber soles, fuzzy cream shearling interior linings, and a decorative white-and-brown braided fabric trim running along the openings.
