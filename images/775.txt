This historical Slavic folk-inspired ensemble features a dominant ivory-beige and dark brown color palette accented with red geometric embroidery, providing full body coverage. The under-layer is a loose, standard-length linen rubakha shirt that rests just slightly below the belt at the upper hips. It features a split V-neckline with a small stand collar and long straight sleeves. Red geometric cross-stitch folk embroidery frames the neckline, sleeve bicep bands, cuffs, and lower hemline. Layered over the shirt is a dark brown, sleeveless leather kozhukh vest adorned with an embossed, elegant tonal damask pattern. A narrow red and brown woven cord belt cinches the natural waist. Beneath the shirt are loose dark trousers tucked into matching dark brown leather boots featuring flat soles.
