This edgy autumn biker outfit features a dominant monochromatic solid black color palette accented with delicate gold jewelry and no handheld bags. The primary top layer is a baggy, loose black knit turtleneck sweater with dropped shoulders and relaxed sleeves. The matching lower layer consists of high-waisted, form-fitting black leggings in a stretchy, matte knit fabric that fully covers the legs down to the ankles. Fine accessories include a delicate gold chain necklace with a clover pendant and a minimalist gold nail-shaped bangle bracelet. Black crew socks are worn on the feet, with the look styled completely without shoes, showing only the socks beneath the leggings.
