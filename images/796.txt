This chic, feminine outfit features a monochromatic cream and white color palette with a light, preppy aesthetic. The dominant upper garment is a loose, slightly oversized cream ribbed-knit polo sweater made from a soft, medium-weight vertical ribbed fabric. It features a wide open spread collar, a deep unbuttoned V-neckline, dropped shoulders, and straight full-length sleeves. On the lower body, high-waisted white tweed tailored shorts provide a structured, clean fit, exposing the entire length of the legs. Jewelry features a butterfly theme, including a delicate gold chain necklace with a butterfly pendant, matching gold butterfly stud earrings, and a thin gold butterfly charm bracelet. Completing the look are flat cream woven fabric Mary Jane shoes with a black contrast toe cap and a single black elastic instep strap.
