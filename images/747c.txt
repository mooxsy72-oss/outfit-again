This casual winter street style outfit features a cohesive tan, brown, and light blue color palette. The primary upper garment is an oversized, heavy-knit cream wool sweater that comfortably covers the torso. It features a thick, structured mock-neck collar, relaxed drop shoulders, and long, loose sleeves that drape softly into wide ribbed cuffs at the wrists. On the lower body, high-waisted light-wash blue denim jeans provide a structured, wide-leg drape to the floor. On the feet are flat, platform cream winter lace-up boots lined with thick shearling.
