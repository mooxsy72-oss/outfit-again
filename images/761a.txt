This traditional Slavic-inspired folk ensemble features a dominant cream and muted olive-green color palette with terracotta-orange and multi-colored embroidered accents. Providing full body coverage, it comprises a loose white linen shirt with voluminous, softly draped bishop sleeves ending in wide, stiff cuffs embroidered with gold, blue, and orange floral scrollwork. Layered over it is a traditional sarafan dress with wide shoulder straps, a square neckline, and a heavily gathered olive-green skirt bordered at the chest and waist by matching multi-colored embroidered bands. A thick, terracotta-orange fabric sash ties snugly at the waist, leaving the head completely bare and free of any scarves or brow-bands, while beautifully displaying the hair. Footwear consists of high, light sand-colored leather boots crafted in an antique, historical style with soft shafts and flat soles, paired with large bronze filigree earrings and ornate stone-encrusted necklaces.
