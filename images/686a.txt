This chic cocktail outfit features a dark brown chiffon mini dress. The body-con mini dress is constructed from a lightweight, sheer chiffon overlay over a solid dark brown lining, creating a form-fitting silhouette that ends mid-thigh. It features an off-the-shoulder construction with a sweetheart neckline and a twisted, ruched bustier design that exposes the collarbones and shoulders. The long, unlined sleeves are semi-sheer, fluidly draping down the arms and flaring slightly at the wrists. Dense horizontal and vertical ruching runs along the torso and sides, creating soft fabric texture and structure. Minimalist accessories include a delicate gold chain necklace, with the look styled completely barefoot. The overall palette is solid, unpatterned dark brown.
