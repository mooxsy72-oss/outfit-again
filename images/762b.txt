This ensemble features a dominant ivory-white palette with secondary terracotta-red accents, adorned with traditional geometric patterns. Providing full body coverage, the core garment is a floor-length, A-line linen dress reminiscent of a traditional sorochka. It features a modest scoop neckline framed by a wide triangular collar panel filled with dense terracotta-red and gold geometric embroidery, long sleeves, and a matching embroidered trim along the lower hem. A wide, structured terracotta-red fabric sash wraps tightly around the waist, topped by a dangling, multi-paneled white fabric belt embellished with geometric borders and a central metal medallion, fully exposing the shoulder and torso silhouette with no outer capes or cloaks, with the look styled completely barefoot beneath the sweeping hemline.
