This look features an all-black, contemporary academia aesthetic, stripped of its heavy outerwear layer and legwear. The primary upper garment is a tight, cropped mock-neck top with long fitted sleeves that snugly hugs the torso, completely revealing the arms and leaving the midriff and navel area exposed with no outer blazers. It pairs with the matching slim-fit, high-waisted mini skirt with a subtle front side slit. Silver heart-shaped drop earrings remain as the only accessory, with the look styled completely barefoot on bare legs, entirely free of any loafers or knee-high socks.
