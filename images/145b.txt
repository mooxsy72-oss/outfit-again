This fantasy Russian boyar-inspired ensemble shifts to a minimalist light blue and gold palette, completely stripped of its heavy ivory outer layers and heavy shoulder jewelry. The primary garment is now a traditional, loose-fitting floor-length under-dress crafted from fluid, lightweight satin fabric woven with gold floral damask patterns. It features a classic, straight and non-contoured silhouette that drapes softly down to the floor without emphasizing the waist, completely opening the neck, collarbones, and shoulders with no collars or overlays. The voluminous, heavily gathered long sleeves taper neatly into tight, pearl-encrusted gold cuffs at the wrists, while the head remains completely bare. Matching flat Mary Jane-style ballet shoes crafted from ivory damask and accented with gold lace trim on a completely flat sole peek slightly from the hem.
