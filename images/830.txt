This contemporary casual outfit features a dominant solid black color distribution paired with a secondary brown and cream gingham check pattern and a single metallic gold accent.
The outermost layer is an oversized, mid-thigh length blazer jacket crafted from thick, structured black faux leather that retains a slightly rigid silhouette. It features classic notched lapels, a single-button waist closure, and long loose sleeves. Beneath it sits a tight, form-fitting cropped t-shirt made of smooth, medium-weight cotton jersey, featuring a high crew neckline, short sleeves, and a straight hem that leaves the midriff exposed.
The lower garment is a high-waisted mini-skirt featuring a dense, small-scale gingham checkered pattern in dark brown and cream. The skirt has a flat wrap-style front waist secured by a single black button, transitioning into crisp, structured knife pleats on the sides and back that end at the upper thigh. On the feet are heavy black leather combat boots with a matte finish, front lacing, and thick, chunky rubber lugged platform soles. Accessorizing the neckline is a delicate, thin gold chain necklace with a small geometric ring pendant.
