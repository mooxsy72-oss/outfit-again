This chic autumn-inspired outfit features a dominant deep burgundy and crisp white color palette. The primary top layer is a loose-fitting, fluffy burgundy knit cardigan made of thick, textured bouclé-style yarn with small patch pockets and straight sleeves. The right chest features curved white embroidered text reading "GELATO PIQUE". Underneath, a fitted white cropped top with a deep V-neckline leaves the collarbones and a strip of the midriff exposed. On the lower body, high-waisted burgundy tailored shorts offer a structured, clean fit, exposing the entire length of the legs. Accessories include a delicate gold chain necklace with a red four-leaf clover pendant, a matching red flower charm bracelet, and red flower stud earrings. Completing the look is a pair of low-heeled burgundy mesh Mary Jane flats with slim instep straps.
