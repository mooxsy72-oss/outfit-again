This modern smart-casual men's ensemble relies on a balanced palette of solid cream-white and dark navy blue, providing near-total body coverage. The upper garment is a relaxed-fit, hip-length sweater crafted from a heavy, thickly textured mélange knit wool with a prominent ribbed stitch pattern. It features a simple round crew neckline, dropped shoulders, long loose sleeves bunched slightly at the wrists, and a relaxed hem loosely tucked at the waist. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, tailored navy blue trousers made of structured matte wool cloth. They feature distinct double front pleats, sharp pressed center creases, a hidden waistband construction, and a straight-leg silhouette terminating in cuffed ankles, with the look styled completely barefoot.
