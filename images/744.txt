This edgy winter streetwear outfit features a dominant monochromatic black leather texture contrasted with a single cream accent. The primary upper layer is a bulky, cropped black faux-leather puffer jacket with thick horizontal quilting, a high insulated stand-up collar, and a toggle-drawstring hem. Underneath, it exposes a small strip of the midriff. The matching lower layer consists of high-waisted, wide-leg black faux-leather trousers that provide a heavy, structured drape pooling slightly at the floor. A contrasting cream knit scarf with long fringed edges is wrapped around the neck. Completing the look is a pair of rugged black leather combat boots featuring thick, lugged platform soles, block heels, and front laces.
