This contemporary casual outfit features an oversized shacket and matching tailored shorts in an intense royal blue hue, paired with strappy stiletto sandals. The heavyweight wool blend or thick felt shacket dominates the silhouette with its loose, relaxed fit, traditional pointed collar, dropped shoulders, and wide sleeves with rolled-up cuffs. It features a center-front button placket with five prominent round gold buttons and two large symmetrical flap pockets on the chest. The shacket covers the torso and upper thighs, partially concealing the matching high-waisted tailored shorts beneath, leaving the legs completely exposed. The black leather sandals feature thin stiletto heels and delicate criss-cross straps that wrap around the instep and tie above the ankles. Multiple stacked chunky gold bracelets on both wrists and large gold hoop earrings complete the look.
