This fantasy Slavic-inspired ritual ensemble features a strict monochrome white palette with prominent metallic silver textures. Providing full body coverage, the core garment is a floor-length, A-line gown crafted from fluidly draping white chiffon. It features a high, round neckline decorated with silver embroidery, openwork geometric eyelet lace across the upper chest, and long, voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into narrow cuffs. A sheer white chiffon caplet rests over the shoulders, gathered into a knot at the center chest. Cinching the waist is a highly structured, wide silver metal belt engraved with ornate Slavic filigree motifs, supporting three draped, cascading silver chains. Concealed entirely beneath the sweeping hemline are matching plain white leather flat boots. A thin silver headband and dangling bronze birdcage earrings complete the look.
