This traditional Slavic folk ensemble features a dominant printed light brown color scheme with secondary white and red accents. Providing full body coverage down to the ankles, the under-layer consists of a white cotton sorochka shirt featuring a gathered scoop neckline and three-quarter length billowy sleeves ending in ruffled cuffs. Layered over it is an ankle-length sarafan jumper dress with a high-waisted, heavily gathered skirt. The mid-weight, crisp cotton fabric is covered in a repeating small-scale pattern of wavy lines, featuring solid red shoulder straps and a solid red horizontal band framing the bodice. A narrow red and white geometric woven poyas belt ties tightly at the waist with long dangling tasseled ends, with the look styled completely barefoot beneath the hemline.
