This casual, everyday outfit features a cohesive cream, light blue, and tan color palette with a subtle retro flair. The dominant upper layer is a slightly oversized cream-colored cable-knit sweater made of a thick, heavy-weight yarn that creates soft volume. It features a ribbed crew neckline, dropped shoulders, and loose sleeves ending in fitted ribbed cuffs. Layered underneath, a white cotton collared shirt peeks out neatly at the neckline. The sweater is tucked into high-waisted, secondary light-wash blue denim jeans featuring a structured fit through the hips that dramatically flares out into voluminous bell-bottoms at the floor-length hem. The waist is accented by a tan braided leather belt with a round silver buckle. On the feet are chunky, flat white platform athletic sneakers.
