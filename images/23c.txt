Traditional Russian royal ensemble consists of a floor-length, A-line velvet caftan-gown, featuring a dominant deep black and metallic gold color distribution. The gown is constructed from heavy, dense black velvet that retains structure and forms deep, vertical drapes, featuring voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into rigid, elongated cuffs. The high mandarin collar has been completely removed, creating a clean neckline that exposes the neck and collarbones without any heavy overlays or embroidery. Symmetrical, medium-scale gold embroidery with fleur-de-lis and floral motifs heavily embellishes the front closure, cuffs, and lower skirt, while the head remains completely bare, with the look styled completely barefoot beneath the sweeping hemline.
