This fantasy Russian folk-inspired ensemble features a minimalist, clean white palette, completely stripped of its heavy outer layers and headwear. The primary garment is now a simple, loose-fitting floor-length under-dress crafted from fluid, lightweight white satin fabric, completely opening the neck, collarbones, and shoulders with no collars or overlays. It features a soft fluid drape that skims the body, and voluminous, heavily gathered long sleeves that taper neatly into tight silver-embroidered cuffs at the wrists, while the head and hands remain completely bare. Closed-toe white leather shoes are concealed beneath the sweeping hemline.
