This traditional Russian folk ensemble shifts to a minimalist and authentic baseline, stripped of its heavy decorative layers. The primary garment is now a traditional, loose-fitting ankle-length white linen rubakha shirt. It features a classic, straight and non-contoured silhouette that drapes softly down to the floor without emphasizing the waist, completely opening the neck and collarbones. It retains its oversized, billowy bishop sleeves gathered at the lace-trimmed cuffs, while the head remains completely bare. On the feet, the woven bast lapti shoes paired with thick white linen leg wraps cross-tied up to the shins are still present.
