This casual streetwear outfit features a neutral grey, black, and white color palette with an oversized sporty silhouette. The dominant garment is a loose, oversized heather grey hoodie made from heavy, structured cotton fleece. It features drastically dropped shoulders, a spacious double-layered hood with thin grey drawstrings, and voluminous sleeves with bunched-up fabric ending in tight ribbed cuffs. A prominent kangaroo pouch pocket sits at the lower front. In sharp contrast, the secondary lower layer consists of tight, skin-tight black biker shorts in a matte, elastic knit fabric that fully covers the hips and upper thighs, leaving the lower legs completely bare. Accessories include slouchy, thick white ribbed cotton socks bunched at the ankles, paired with chunky white and grey athletic dad sneakers featuring thick, sculpted multi-panel soles.
