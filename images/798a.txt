This cozy autumn outfit features a warm beige and dark brown color palette with no handheld accessories. The dominant upper garment is a loose, oversized beige ribbed-knit sweater made of a thick, heavy-weight yarn, featuring an exaggerated, voluminous turtleneck collar, dropped shoulders, and loose balloon sleeves with tight ribbed cuffs. It is loosely tucked into a high-waisted, secondary pair of dark brown leather shorts with sharp front pleats, secured by a matching thick leather belt with a rectangular buckle. Transparent sheer black tights cover the legs down to the feet, layered underneath bunched-up beige knit socks that are worn around the ankles. The look is styled entirely without shoes, showing only the soft, thick knit socks over the sheer hosiery on the feet.
