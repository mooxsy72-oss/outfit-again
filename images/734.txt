This monochromatic, dark chocolate brown autumn ensemble features an oversized biker jacket, a ruched column dress, and matching block-heel ankle boots. The oversized leather or polyurethane biker jacket creates a voluminous upper silhouette, constructed with wide panelled sleeves, drop shoulders, wide notched lapels with gold-tone metal snaps, an asymmetrical front zipper, and a built-in belt at the hem. Layered beneath is a form-fitting sleeveless midi dress made from a soft, high-stretch jersey or fine knit fabric. It features a high crew neckline, clean armholes, and subtle horizontal ruching tightly gathered across the natural waistline, hugging the torso down to a straight calf-length hem. Completing the outfit are smooth brown leather ankle boots with structured square toes, tight-fitting shafts, and thick, sculpted block high heels.
