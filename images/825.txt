This modern, feminine outfit features a dominant light lavender color paired with a secondary crisp white, presenting a clean and solid aesthetic without visual patterns.
The inner layer is a tight, white strapless crop top made of a smooth, elastic knit fabric that leaves the shoulders, collarbones, and a wide section of the midriff exposed. Over it rests an oversized, unbuttoned lavender shirt-jacket crafted from medium-weight satin or fluid silk, creating a voluminous drape. It features a classic collar, drop-shoulder seams, and loose sleeves that are gathered at the cuffs into large, structured fabric bows.
A matching high-waisted lavender mini skirt completes the silhouette. Made from a stiffer, structured denim or heavy cotton fabric that retains its A-line shape, it features a prominent vertical lace-up detail with thin cords on the left thigh. On the feet are minimalist white heeled sandals with thin ankle ties that cross over the lower leg.
