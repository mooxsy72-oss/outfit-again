This fantasy Slavic-inspired ritual ensemble features a strict monochrome white palette, stripped of its heavy metallic details. Providing full body coverage, the core garment is a floor-length, A-line gown crafted from fluidly draping white chiffon. It features a high, round neckline decorated with silver embroidery, openwork geometric eyelet lace across the upper chest, and long, voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into narrow cuffs, fully exposing the upper bodice and shoulders with no outer caplets. The waist and torso are left completely un-cinched and free of any rigid silver metal belts or cascading chains, allowing the loose chiffon gown to fall in a completely natural, straight, and un-contoured historical silhouette, with the look styled completely barefoot beneath the sweeping hemline.
