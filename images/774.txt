This traditional East Slavic rural winter ensemble features an earthy palette dominated by textured dark brown and medium grey-brown tones, accented by cream-white, providing total body coverage against the cold. The base headwear is an antique-style cream-white knit wool cap that fits closely to the head with a distinct, densely ribbed historical texture. Worn directly over this cap is a deep hood formed by a massive, highly voluminous grey-brown downy wool shawl. Woven from fuzzy goat down, this shawl features openwork lace patterns along its scalloped edges, wrapping heavily around the head and shoulders like a bulky cape, secured at the chest by a dark clasp. Layered beneath is a thick, boxy dark brown sheepskin coat (polushubok) with heavy straight sleeves. The look finishes with traditional grey-brown felted wool boots (valenki).
