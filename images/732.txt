This evening ensemble features a black corset top paired with sparkling black wide-leg pants and mesh stiletto sandals. The form-fitting corset top is crafted from stiff, structured fabric with vertical boning seams, a square neckline, thick shoulder straps, and a pointed asymmetrical hem that exposes the midriff. It pairs with high-waisted, rigid black denim or heavy cotton pants that feature a relaxed, wide-leg silhouette. The pants are densely adorned with an all-over pattern of tiny, shimmering heat-set rhinestones. Black high-heeled sandals feature a thin stiletto heel, a delicate ankle strap, and sheer mesh panels across the open toe. Accessories include a delicate gold chain necklace and matching drop earrings, both featuring black four-leaf clover motifs.
