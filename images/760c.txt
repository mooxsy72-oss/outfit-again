This cozy, preppy autumn outfit features a clean white and plaid color palette, stripped of its heavy knitwear layer. The primary upper garment is a fitted, lightweight white cotton crewneck t-shirt with short fitted sleeves that snugly hugs the torso, completely revealing the arms and creating a streamlined, minimalist upper silhouette. It is neatly tucked into the high waistline of the A-line mini skirt, which is crafted from thick, structured wool-blend fabric with a dense plaid pattern in shades of brown, beige, and black, with the look styled completely barefoot.
