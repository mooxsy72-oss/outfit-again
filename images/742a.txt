This dark winter streetwear outfit features a monochromatic charcoal grey and solid black color palette with an oversized silhouette. The outermost layer is a long, oversized charcoal grey wool-blend coat with a heavy texture, sharp notched lapels, and a relaxed double-breasted button closure. Underneath sits a baggy, loose black knit turtleneck sweater with dropped shoulders and relaxed sleeves. The secondary lower layer consists of skin-tight black leggings that fully cover the legs down to the ankles. Accessories include a long black knit scarf with fringed edges. Thick, grey ribbed-knit socks peek out above rugged black leather combat boots featuring thick, lugged platform soles and front laces.
