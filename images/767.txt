This traditional Slavic folk-inspired costume features a balanced palette of ivory-grey and deep red, decorated with red and black geometric embroidery. Providing full body coverage, the under-layer is a floor-length linen tunic or sorochka in ivory-grey with voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into patterned red cuffs. The modest split round neckline features a dark red border band, flanked by red and black cross-stitch panels. Red star motifs line the sleeves. Layered over the flanks is a split, floor-length red plakhta wrap skirt. A narrow, red and white woven fabric belt (poyas) wraps multiple times around the waist, knotted with long dangling ends down the center front. Concealed entirely beneath the sweeping hemline are matching deep red leather boots with flat soles.
