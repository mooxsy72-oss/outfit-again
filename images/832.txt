This military-inspired contemporary streetwear outfit features a monochrome color distribution dominated by varying shades of olive and khaki green, balanced by secondary off-white footwear and metallic gold accents.
The outermost layer is a cropped, structured jacket crafted from smooth, semi-matte olive green faux leather. It features a classic pointed collar, dropped shoulders, long straight sleeves with plain cuffs, and welt side pockets, remaining unzipped at the front. Beneath it sits a tight, form-fitting cropped tank top made of a lightweight, ribbed knit fabric in a matching olive green tone, featuring a simple crew neckline and leaving a narrow strip of the midriff exposed.
The lower garment consists of high-waisted, relaxed-fit denim shorts in a washed khaki-green hue. They feature classic five-pocket construction, a zippered fly with a top button, and raw, lightly frayed hems that end at the upper thigh, fully exposing the legs. On the feet are bulky, multi-paneled retro dad sneakers in off-white leather and mesh, featuring thick, sculpted platform rubber soles with a silver logo accent on the sides. Wearable accessories include a delicate gold chain necklace with a small geometric pendant and a chunky, heavy gold link chain bracelet wrapped around the right wrist.
