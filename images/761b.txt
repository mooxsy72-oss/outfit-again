This traditional Slavic-inspired folk ensemble features a dominant cream and muted olive-green color palette with terracotta-orange and multi-colored embroidered accents. Providing full body coverage, it comprises a loose white linen shirt with voluminous, softly draped bishop sleeves ending in wide, stiff cuffs embroidered with gold, blue, and orange floral scrollwork. Layered over it is a traditional sarafan dress with wide shoulder straps, a square neckline, and a heavily gathered olive-green skirt bordered at the chest and waist by matching multi-colored embroidered bands. A thick, terracotta-orange fabric sash ties snugly at the waist, leaving the head completely bare and free of any scarves or brow-bands. The look maintains the large bronze filigree earrings and ornate stone-encrusted necklaces, styled completely barefoot.
