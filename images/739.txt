This casual, laid-back summer outfit features a relaxed beige, white, and light-blue denim color palette with an elongated leg line. The primary layering piece is a loose, oversized beige knit cardigan made from a soft, medium-weight ribbed fabric, featuring a deep V-neckline, unbuttoned front with round brown buttons, and relaxed sleeves hanging loosely off one shoulder. Underneath, a tight white cropped tank top leaves the midriff, collarbones, and chest exposed. The secondary lower layer is a high-waisted light-wash blue denim mini-skirt with a straight, rigid structure and a raw, frayed hemline that exposes the entire length of the bare legs. Accessories are limited to a delicate gold chain necklace with a small pendant. Completing the look is a pair of classic low-top white leather athletic sneakers with matching white laces.
