This casual streetwear outfit features a defined contrast in volume, combining a layered cropped white top section with fluid, oversized dark olive green trousers, excluding any sunglasses or handbag.
The upper layers consist of a form-fitting white cropped camisole with a thin shoulder strap, layered beneath an ultra-cropped white open-knit sweater shrug. The shrug features an asymmetric off-the-shoulder neckline, exposing the collarbones and left shoulder, and voluminous long bishop sleeves gathered into wide ribbed cuffs. The lower body is defined by exaggeratedly oversized, wide-leg dark olive green trousers crafted from smooth, flowing woven fabric with a gathered elastic waistband and a puddle hem.
On the feet are classic low-top white Nike leather sneakers with clean lines and flat white rubber soles.
