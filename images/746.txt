This elegant evening outfit features a monochromatic champagne-gold color palette with a fluid, fluidly draping silhouette. The single dominating garment is a silk satin slip maxi dress with a bias-cut construction that closely hugs the torso and hips. It features an open, draped cowl neckline, ultra-thin spaghetti straps, and a low, completely open back that leaves the shoulders, chest, and arms exposed. A dramatic, high thigh-slit cuts upward along the right side of the ankle-length skirt, exposing the entire right leg. Minimalist secondary accessories include a delicate gold pendant chain necklace, a thin gold chain bracelet, and matching drop earrings. On the feet are matching champagne-gold leather stiletto sandals featuring narrow ankle straps and open toes.
