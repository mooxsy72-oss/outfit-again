This modern monochromatic lounge ensemble features a uniform pastel peach color palette defined by a highly distinctive, ultra-fuzzy mohair-imitation texture. The inner layer is a form-fitting, cropped camisole top with a straight neckline and thin spaghetti shoulder straps, leaving the lower midriff exposed. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, baggy wide-leg trousers featuring a gathered elastic waistband, with the voluminous fabric pooling softly over the feet. Layered on top is a knee-length, oversized cardigan coat worn slouchy and completely off the left shoulder, featuring long loose sleeves, open-front construction, and a wide notched lapel collar. Footwear consists of contrasting crisp white open-toe slide slippers featuring plush faux-fur straps. A delicate, thin gold chain necklace with a small circular pendant sits over the collarbones.
