This classic preppy casual outfit features a balanced color distribution dominated by a solid charcoal grey and matching light blue tones, accented by cream-beige footwear and gold hardware.
The inner layer is a long-sleeved button-down shirt crafted from lightweight, smooth cotton in a solid light denim-blue. It features a sharp, structured pointed collar and long cuffs that peek out from the layer above. Over it sits a cropped, relaxed-fit crewneck sweater knitted from a thick, soft charcoal-grey wool blend. It has dropped shoulders and wide ribbed edges along the neckline, hem, and cuffs, allowing the long curved hem of the blue shirt to extend well past the sweater's waistline.
The lower half consists of high-waisted, straight-leg jeans made of thick, structured light-wash blue denim that matches the shirt. They feature classic five-pocket styling and a clean, unpolished texture. On the feet are chunky, multi-paneled retro sneakers in a mix of cream, beige, and white mesh and suede. A classic gold metal wristwatch with a link bracelet finishes the look.
