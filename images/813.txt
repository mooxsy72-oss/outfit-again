This winter streetwear outfit features a striking contrast between crisp white and vibrant lime-green elements. The dominant, silhouette-defining upper layer is an oversized, voluminous white puffer jacket constructed from thick, horizontally quilted fabric with subtle vertical ribbing. It features a high, insulated stand-up collar, zip-front closure, and long, puffy sleeves with elasticated cuffs that completely cover the arms. On the lower body, straight-leg lime-green denim trousers offer a structured, relaxed fit, cropping slightly above the ankles. Accessories include a matching lime-green ribbed-knit beanie hat worn high on the head. Completing the look is a pair of rugged white leather combat boots with thick, lugged platform soles and functional white laces.
