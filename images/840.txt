This casual everyday streetwear outfit features a well-balanced color palette blending earthy brown tones with classic blue denim and solid white accents.
The upper layer is an oversized, relaxed-fit dark brown corduroy shirt worn open as a light jacket. It is crafted from medium-weight structured cotton fabric with a faint vertical ribbed texture, featuring a pointed collar, dropped shoulders, and loose sleeves loosely cuffed at the mid-forearms. Underneath is a form-fitting white tank top with a deep scoop neckline, exposing the collarbones and neck. The lower body is defined by high-waisted, straight-leg light blue denim jeans that drape fluidly to the ankles, secured by a brown leather belt with a metallic buckle.
On the feet are classic low-top white leather sneakers with flat soles.
