This traditional Russian folk ensemble shifts to a minimalist and authentic baseline, stripped of its heavy decorative layers. The primary garment is a traditional, loose-fitting ankle-length white linen rubakha shirt. It features a classic, straight and non-contoured silhouette that drapes softly down to the floor without emphasizing the waist, completely opening the neck and collarbones, and keeping its oversized, billowy bishop sleeves. The head remains completely bare, with the look styled completely barefoot on bare legs, free of any shoes, wraps, or leg ties.
