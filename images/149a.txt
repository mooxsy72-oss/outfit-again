This fantasy Russian boyar-inspired ensemble features a dominant rose-pink and gold palette with cream secondary accents. It features a wide decorative gold border down the center front split and long, slit cape-like hanging sleeves. Peeking out from underneath are the voluminous, gathered cream satin sleeves of an under-shirt, fitted into tight cuffs. A large, circular golden barmy collar set with pearls rests over the shoulders, leaving the head completely bare without any headdresses or hair ornaments. Rounded brown leather shoes are minimally visible at the hem.
