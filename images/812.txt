This modern streetwear casual outfit is heavily dominated by an oversized, boxy black hockey jersey made from mid-weight, structured athletic mesh fabric. The jersey features a deep V-neckline, drastically dropped shoulders, and wide, loose sleeves ending in fitted ribbed cuffs. It is heavily printed with white graphic elements, including the "Ghostface" mask and a knife on the front, text reading "Ghost face.", smaller masks on the shoulders, and horizontal athletic stripes on the sleeves and hem. In stark contrast to the voluminous top, the secondary lower layer consists of tight, form-fitting black leggings in a stretchy, matte knit fabric that fully covers the legs. Completing the look is a pair of low-top light grey and white sneakers with black swoosh logos and red tongue details.
