This autumn-inspired casual outfit features a balanced color distribution dominated by rich brown tones, a secondary solid black, and a classic plaid pattern.
The base layer is a tight, form-fitting turtleneck top in solid black, made from a smooth, lightweight ribbed knit fabric that covers the entire torso, neck, and long sleeves down to the wrists. Layered loosely over it is an oversized, long-sleeved button-up shirt crafted from medium-weight, soft cotton flannel. The shirt features a dense brown, beige, and cream plaid pattern, a classic pointed collar, and white buttons.
The lower garment consists of high-waisted, voluminous shorts made from thick, structured brown corduroy fabric with visible vertical ribs. They feature a gathered paperbag waist construction cinched by a narrow black leather belt with a gold square buckle, and cuffed hems that end at the mid-thigh. On the feet are chunky black leather chelsea boots featuring prominent elastic side panels and thick, heavily lugged platform rubber soles.
