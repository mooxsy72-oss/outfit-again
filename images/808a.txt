This feminine, summery outfit features a clean cream and white color palette with a light, preppy aesthetic. The dominant upper garment is a cropped cream knit cardigan with a round crew neckline, a front button closure with round fabric-covered buttons, and short puff sleeves gathered at the shoulders. The soft, fine-gauge knit fabric is detailed with scattered, small embroidered bird motifs in blue, yellow, and brown tones. On the lower body, a high-waisted white denim mini-skirt provides a structured, straight fit that leaves the entire length of the legs exposed. Accessories include a silver butterfly pendant necklace, a white clover-shaped bracelet, and small pearl stud earrings, leaving the head and hair completely free of any headbands, caps, or hair bands. On the feet are white leather slide sandals featuring a signature H-shaped cutout strap and modest block heels.
