This historical Slavic folk-inspired ensemble features a dominant ivory-beige and dark brown color palette accented with red geometric embroidery. The primary upper garment is a loose, standard-length linen rubakha shirt that rests just down to the upper hips, fully visible with its torso left completely un-cinched and free of any leather vests, cords, or fabric sashes. Red geometric cross-stitch folk embroidery frames the neckline, sleeve bicep bands, cuffs, and lower hemline. Beneath the shirt are loose dark trousers tucked into matching dark brown leather boots featuring flat soles.
