This chic, modern smart-casual outfit features a monochromatic black and cream palette, stripped of its heavy outerwear layer. The primary upper garment is a tight-fitting, ribbed knit cream turtleneck sweater with long fitted sleeves that closely hugs the torso, creating a streamlined and clean upper silhouette. It is tucked cleanly into the high-waisted band of smooth, matte black straight-leg trousers. Accessories include a short silver chain necklace holding a small round medallion pendant, while the head remains completely bare, and footwear consists of low-profile, glossy black patent leather penny loafers with thin soles and rounded toes.
