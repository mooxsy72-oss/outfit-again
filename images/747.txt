This casual winter street style outfit features a cohesive tan, brown, and light blue color palette with an insulated, streamlined silhouette. The dominant upper garment is a bulky, cropped brown puffer jacket featuring thick horizontal quilting and a high, fully zipped-up stand-up collar that completely covers the torso, neck, and throat. On the lower body, high-waisted light-wash blue denim jeans provide a structured, wide-leg drape to the floor. Accessories include a plush brown cat-ear beanie hat worn on the head and a rectangular cream shearling-trimmed crossbody bag worn over the closed jacket. On the feet are flat, platform cream winter lace-up boots lined with thick shearling.
