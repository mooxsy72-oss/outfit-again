This preppy casual outfit features a balanced neutral color distribution dominated by a brown plaid pattern and a solid crisp white tone, accented by deep tan-brown leather.
The upper garment is a sleeveless, form-fitting turtleneck top in solid white, made from a medium-weight ribbed knit jersey that contours smoothly to the torso. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, tailored shorts crafted from a thick wool blend fabric featuring a dense, small-scale brown and tan houndstooth plaid pattern. They feature a flat front waistband and a straight silhouette ending at the upper thigh.
The legs are styled with white knit over-the-knee socks. On the feet are tan-brown leather Mary Jane pumps featuring a block heel and a single instep strap. Wearable accessories include a delicate gold chain necklace and small gold hoop earrings.
