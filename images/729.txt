This two-piece loungewear set consists of a matching short-sleeve button-down shirt and shorts in a solid jet black color. Crafted from a lightweight, fluidly draping satin fabric with a high-shine finish, the regular-fit shirt features a classic notched lapel collar, a front button placket with four small dark buttons, and a single patch pocket on the left chest. The matching mid-rise shorts have a relaxed, flared leg silhouette with a tightly gathered elastic waistband. The entire ensemble features thin contrast white piping along the collar edges, center placket, shirt sleeve cuffs, chest pocket rim, and the bottom hems of the shorts, crisply framing the monochromatic design.
