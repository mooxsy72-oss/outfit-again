This modern cozy autumn athleisure look features a dominant palette of khaki-brown and tan tones, accented by a solid cream base layer. The primary upper garment is the skin-tight, cropped white sports camisole with a square scoop neckline and thin elastic straps, smoothly hugging the torso and completely exposing the shoulders, arms, and midriff area. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, form-fitting khaki-brown leggings crafted from a smooth, highly elastic compressive performance fabric. Thick, slouched cream knit crew socks with a ribbed texture are worn on the feet, with the look styled completely without shoes, showing only the plush socks beneath the leggings.
