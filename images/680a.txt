This sleek evening look is composed of a white crop top, a high-waisted black leather midi skirt, and elegant black high heels. The simple white jersey crop top serves as the primary upper layer, featuring a sleeveless construction that fully exposes the arms, shoulders, and midriff. It is tucked into a structured, matte black faux-leather midi skirt. This pencil-style skirt features a clean, high waistband anchored by a slim black leather belt with a dark rectangular buckle, and it is defined by a bold, dramatic front thigh-high slit that exposes the left leg. The silhouette is lengthened by classic black leather stiletto high heels. The color scheme is a sharp, solid contrast of white and black.
