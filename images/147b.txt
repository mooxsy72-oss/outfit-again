This fantasy Russian boyar-inspired ensemble features a clean burgundy, gold, and cream palette, stripped of its heavy outerwear. The outfit centers entirely on the floor-length, A-line sarafan dress made of heavy, structured burgundy brocade woven with a large gold floral damask pattern, featuring a buttoned central gold trim band, completely revealing the upper body and sleeve silhouette. Underneath is the cream satin shirt with voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into tight, gold-embroidered cuffs, while the head remains completely bare. Matching closed-toe dark brown leather shoes are entirely concealed beneath the sweeping hemline.
