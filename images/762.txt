This ensemble features a dominant ivory-white palette with secondary terracotta-red and dark brown accents, adorned with traditional geometric patterns. Providing full body coverage, the core garment is a floor-length, A-line linen dress reminiscent of a traditional sorochka. It features a modest scoop neckline framed by a wide triangular collar panel filled with dense terracotta-red and gold geometric embroidery, long sleeves, and a matching embroidered trim along the lower hem. A wide, structured terracotta-red fabric sash wraps tightly around the waist, topped by a dangling, multi-paneled white fabric belt embellished with geometric borders and a central metal medallion. A heavy, oversized dark brown fur-trimmed cloak rests over the shoulders. Concealed entirely beneath the sweeping hemline are matching dark brown leather folk boots with flat soles.
