This traditional East Slavic winter ensemble features a dominant ivory-beige palette with secondary terracotta-red accents and multicolored floral motifs, providing full body coverage. The outermost layer is a calf-length, structured shuba winter coat made of thick ivory wool damask with a subtle self-patterned weave. It features long straight sleeves, a high stand collar, and plush white faux-fur trim along the cuffs, vertical closure placket, and hemline. Terracotta-red geometric cross-stitch embroidery frames the sleeve forearms and lower skirt border. A wide, patterned red and white woven fabric sash belt wraps the waist, knotted with long tassels dangling down the front. Layered beneath the coat is an ankle-length red wool-blend skirt with an embroidered geometric border at the hemline, leaving the head, face, and hands completely bare and free of any shawls, wraps, or mittens. Footwear consists of mid-calf light brown leather boots trimmed with white wool fleece cuffs at the top, featuring flat soles, rounded toes, and delicate red folk-style embroidery stitched along the front shafts.
