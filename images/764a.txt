This historical Northern Slavic peasant-inspired ensemble features a solid, unpatterned palette dominated by matte black with a stark white secondary layer. Providing full body coverage, the under-layer is a loose white linen under-shirt featuring a modest square neckline and long, voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into narrow wrist cuffs. Layered over it is a traditional floor-length black linen sarafan dress with thick shoulder straps and a high square neckline, fully exposing the front panel of the black dress without any aprons, tied fabric layers, or waist overlays. Concealed entirely beneath the heavy sweeping hemlines are matching dark brown leather folk boots with flat soles.
