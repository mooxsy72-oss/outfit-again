This Slavic folk-inspired ensemble features a dominant ivory-beige color with secondary terracotta-orange accents, adorned with traditional geometric cross-stitch embroidery. The core garment is a mid-calf length, long-sleeved dress reminiscent of a traditional sorochka, crafted from lightweight, fluidly draping linen with soft shape retention. It features a deep V-neckline framed by geometric embroidered bands, voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into narrow embroidered cuffs, and a gathered skirt. A wide, textured terracotta-orange fabric sash (poyas) wraps tightly multiple times around the waist, tying in a knot with long tasseled ends hanging down the front. The garment leaves the lower calves and feet exposed, with the look styled completely barefoot.
