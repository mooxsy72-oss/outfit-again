This contemporary casual outfit features a balanced neutral color distribution dominated by a prominent ombré gradient of black, grey, and white, paired with secondary crisp white tones.
The outermost layer is a relaxed-fit, V-neck cardigan knitted from a thick, fuzzy mohair-blend yarn. It features a striking horizontal ombré gradient pattern, shifting fluidly from solid black at the shoulders down to charcoal grey, light grey, and finishing with a crisp white ribbed hem and cuffs. The cardigan remains unbuttoned, secured loosely by transparent or silver buttons. Beneath it is a form-fitting mock-neck top in solid heather grey, made from a smooth, lightweight knit fabric that covers the chest and neck.
The lower garment consists of high-waisted, tailored shorts in structured, mid-weight white suiting fabric or thick denim. They feature a clean waistband, vertical pressed creases along the front legs, and thick, structured cuffed hems that end at the upper thigh, exposing the legs. On the feet are low-top white leather sneakers with grey accents and white laces. Accessories include a thin gold chain necklace with a small black four-leaf clover pendant and a matching black clover link bracelet on the right wrist.
