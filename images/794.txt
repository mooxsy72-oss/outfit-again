This smart-casual outfit features a balanced grey, black, and white color palette with layered textures. The primary top layer is a tight, form-fitting white cropped t-shirt made of smooth, stretchy cotton jersey, featuring a high crew neckline and short sleeves that leave a strip of the midriff exposed. Layered over it is an oversized dark grey tailored blazer crafted from mid-weight woven fabric with sharp notched lapels, a single-button front closure, and flap pockets. The lower body features a high-waisted black wrap mini-skirt with a straight, rigid structure and a small decorative brown button at the waistline. Semitranslucent sheer black tights cover the elongated legs down to the feet, layered underneath contrasting slouchy white ribbed-knit mid-calf socks. Completing the look are flat, structured black leather Chelsea boots featuring thick, lugged platform soles and signature elastic side panels.
