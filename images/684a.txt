This sleek, graphic outfit features a fine leopard-print turtleneck top tucked into a high-waisted white maxi skirt. The long-sleeve top is crafted from a tight, lightweight jersey knit that hugs the bodice and arms, featuring a high mock turtleneck collar. The fabric is entirely covered in a miniature leopard print with small, densely packed black and brown rosettes. The top tucks into a heavy, structured white woven fabric maxi skirt that holds its shape. The skirt features a high waistline cinched by a thick black leather belt with a polished gold square buckle, and it is defined by a bold, dramatic front slit that exposes the left leg. Large, geometric gold statement earrings anchor the look. The color palette relies on a graphic balance of small-scale leopard print, solid white, and gold accents. All footwear and socks are completely removed, leaving the feet entirely bare.
