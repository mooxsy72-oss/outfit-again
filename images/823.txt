This modern fantasy outfit features a crisp white t-shirt dress as the base layer, crafted from a medium-weight, smooth cotton-blend jersey that drapes softly into a relaxed, oversized silhouette. The dress has a crew neckline, loose short sleeves extending near the elbows, and a straight hem ending at the mid-thigh.

This structured piece is made of stiff, metallic-sheen faux leather with a subtle snakeskin texture, featuring a pointed top and bottom edge, vertical boning channels, and a silver metal lace-up front closure.
Matching emerald green over-the-knee boots, made from form-fitting suede with structured block heels, tightly hug the calves and thighs, leaving only a small strip of bare skin exposed.
