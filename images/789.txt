This modern resort-classic menswear ensemble balances a dominant warm taupe-brown color scheme with a crisp white secondary tone, showcasing a classic vertical striped pattern. The upper garment is a relaxed-fit linen button-down shirt featuring a wide camp collar worn open to expose the collarbones and upper chest, long loose sleeves, and a patch pocket on the left breast. The lightweight, breathable linen fabric is printed with vertical white and taupe-brown stripes of medium scale and uniform density. The shirt is cleanly tucked into the high-waisted band of tailored Gurkha trousers made of a structured, slubby taupe-brown linen fabric. They feature prominent double front pleats, sharp pressed center creases, and a wide waistband with an integrated fabric belt fastened by a dark metallic D-ring buckle on the left side. Footwear consists of matching sand-colored penny loafers featuring low stacked heels and almond toes.
