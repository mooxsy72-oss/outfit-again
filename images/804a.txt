This casual summer streetwear outfit features a balanced olive green and white color palette. The primary upper garment is a form-fitting olive green camisole bodysuit featuring a square neckline and ultra-thin spaghetti straps, leaving the shoulders, arms, chest, and collarbones completely exposed, styled free of any outer shirts, jackets, or draped layers. On the lower body, high-waisted olive green tailored shorts made from a structured, mid-weight fabric feature front pleats and side pockets, exposing the full length of the legs. The ensemble features no jewelry or wearable accessories. Completing the look is a pair of classic low-top white leather sneakers with matching white laces and thick flat soles.
