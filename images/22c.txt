This traditional boyar-style outfit provides coverage using layered garments in an off-white, gold, and muted red color scheme, stripped of its heavy winter outerwear. The outfit centers entirely on the core A-line, floor-length sundress-like sarafan made of stiff, patterned jacquard featuring large-scale red and green floral motifs. A vertical gold decorative band inset with oval ruby gems runs down the center front. Underneath, the loose, sheer white silk under-shirt features voluminous puffed sleeves gathered into long, rigid green and gold embroidered cuffs. Accessories include layered pearl necklaces, while the head, neck, and shoulders remain completely bare of any caps, collars, or capes, with the look styled completely barefoot beneath the sweeping hemline.
