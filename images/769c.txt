This traditional East Slavic ensemble shifts to an indoor or mid-season look, stripped of its heavy winter outerwear. The primary garment is a traditional, floor-length Slavic dress-sarafan, consisting of a loose-fitting white linen under-shirt featuring long, loose straight sleeves with thin terracotta-red geometric borders, beneath a structured, deep terracotta-red wool-blend pinafore dress with a classic straight and non-contoured historical silhouette. The bodice features an intricate central chest panel heavily detailed with multi-colored traditional floral embroidery, while a matching wide geometric border frames the floor-length hem. A wide, patterned red and white woven fabric sash belt wraps tightly around the waist, knotted with long tassels dangling down the front. The head, neck, and hands remain completely bare, with the look styled completely barefoot beneath the sweeping hemline.
