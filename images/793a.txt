This modern evening outfit features a voluminous, off-the-shoulder black satin blouse tucked into a high-waisted leopard-print mini skirt, worn over patterned fishnet tights and paired with stiletto pumps. The fluid blouse dominates the upper silhouette with extreme, billowy balloon sleeves gathered into tight cuffs, exposing the right collarbone, shoulder, and neckline, leaving the chest, neck, and wrists completely bare and free of any gold chain necklaces or wristwatches. The structured mini skirt, made of mid-weight jacquard or heavyweight cotton with a dense all-over brown and black leopard pattern, creates a distinct A-line shape. Fishnet tights with an integrated cheetah-spot motif cover the entire legs down to the black patent leather high-heeled pumps.
