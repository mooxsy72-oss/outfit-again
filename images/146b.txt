This fantasy Russian folk-inspired ensemble features a dominant ice-blue and silver palette paired with stark white secondary accents. Providing full body coverage, the floor-length, A-line opashon coat is made of structured ice-blue damask fabric woven with large silver floral brocade patterns, featuring an embroidered central silver border band. It has long, slit cape-like hanging sleeves, revealing the voluminous, gathered white satin sleeves of an under-shirt tucked into tight silver-embroidered cuffs. Plush white faux-fur trims the high collar and coat hem, leaving the head completely bare without any headdresses or hair ornaments, and the hands remain free. Closed-toe white leather shoes are concealed beneath the sweeping hemline.
