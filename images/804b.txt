This casual summer streetwear outfit features a uniform olive green color palette. The primary upper garment is a form-fitting olive green camisole bodysuit featuring a square neckline and ultra-thin spaghetti straps, leaving the shoulders, arms, chest, and collarbones completely exposed. On the lower body, high-waisted olive green tailored shorts made from a structured, mid-weight fabric feature front pleats and side pockets, exposing the full length of the legs. The ensemble features no jewelry or wearable accessories, with the look styled completely barefoot.
