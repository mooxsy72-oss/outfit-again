This casual autumn streetwear outfit features a soft tan, white, and light-blue denim color palette with a relaxed silhouette. The outermost layer is a bulky, cropped tan faux-suede aviator jacket with a wide cream shearling lapel collar, matching plush fur trim on the hem and cuffs, silver zippers, and a dangling waist belt. Underneath, a tight white ribbed-knit cropped t-shirt with raglan short sleeves and a crew neckline hugs the torso. The dominant lower layer consists of high-waisted, light-wash blue denim jeans featuring straight wide legs with sharp horizontal knee rips and a structured floor-length drape. A gold clover-motif charm chain bracelet is worn on the wrist. Completing the look are bi-color tan and white low-top leather athletic sneakers with white laces.
