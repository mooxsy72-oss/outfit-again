This casual, laid-back summer outfit features a relaxed white and light-blue denim color palette with an elongated leg line. The primary top layer is a tight white cropped tank top that leaves the shoulders, midriff, collarbones, and chest exposed. The secondary lower layer is a high-waisted light-wash blue denim mini-skirt with a straight, rigid structure and a raw, frayed hemline that exposes the entire length of the bare legs. Accessories are limited to a delicate gold chain necklace with a small pendant. Completing the look is a pair of classic low-top white leather athletic sneakers with matching white laces.
