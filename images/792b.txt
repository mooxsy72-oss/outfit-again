This look features an all-black, contemporary academia aesthetic, stripped of its heavy outerwear layer. The primary upper garment is a tight, cropped mock-neck top with long fitted sleeves that snugly hugs the torso, completely revealing the arms and leaving the midriff and navel area exposed with no outer blazers or jackets. It pairs with the matching slim-fit, high-waisted mini skirt with a subtle front side slit. The legs are partially covered by sheer, knee-high black socks, and on the feet are chunky, thick-soled leather loafers featuring a distinctive silver triangular emblem on the tongue. Silver heart-shaped drop earrings complete the ensemble.
