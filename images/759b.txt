This modern edgy outfit balances a dominant black color scheme with a secondary crisp white layer, stripped of its leather outerwear. The primary silhouette centers on the oversized, mid-thigh length white poplin shirt dress featuring a classic pointed collar, plunging unbuttoned V-neckline, and long sleeves with wide white cuffs, completely revealing the upper bodice and arms with no outer jackets. Layered firmly over the torso is the highly structured, rigid matte black leather underbust corset featuring vertical boning and a pointed base that sharply defines the waist. The outfit leaves the lower thighs and legs exposed, finishing with mid-calf black leather combat boots with thick platform lug soles and front laces. Wearable accessories include a thick black velvet choker holding a large silver heart pendant.
