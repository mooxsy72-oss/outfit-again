This feminine winter outfit features a cozy coquette-style aesthetic with a grey and black color distribution. The dominant garment is an oversized, chunky grey cable-knit sweater featuring a high turtleneck collar, dropped shoulders, and loose bell sleeves ending in ribbed cuffs. The thick, structured knit fabric is decorated with scattered, small black satin bows stitched symmetrically onto the torso and sleeves. Tight, form-fitting black leggings create a stark textural contrast, fully covering the legs down to the ankles. Adorning the feet are matching grey cable-knit leg warmers, which feature a slouchy, voluminous texture and a single black ribbon bow tied at the top edge, worn entirely without shoes, showing only the soft knit leg warmers on the feet.
