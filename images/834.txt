This romantic, feminine casual outfit features a dominant solid cherry-red color distribution paired with a secondary crisp off-white tone and a small-scale recurring motif.
The primary upper garment is a short-sleeved knit cardigan in solid red, crafted from a medium-weight, smooth compact-knit fabric that retains its shape. It features a high crew neckline, straight short sleeves, and a button-front closure adorned with small, polished white pearl buttons. The silhouette cinches gently at the waist before flaring out into a dramatic, voluminous peplum hem with fluid ruffles.
The lower garment consists of high-waisted, straight-leg jeans made of thick, structured off-white denim. The pants feature an all-over, scattered embroidery pattern of small red hearts spaced evenly across the fabric. On the feet are matching bright red patent-leather mule heels with a pointed toe and slim stiletto heels. Wearable accessories include a small, structured shoulder bag entirely constructed from glossy red spherical beads shaped into a heart, and a thick, rigid cream-colored resin cuff bracelet.
