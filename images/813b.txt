This winter streetwear outfit features a vibrant and streamlined lime-green and white color palette, stripped of its bulky outerwear. The primary upper garment is now a lightweight, tight-fitting lime-green sweater crafted from fine-gauge knit fabric. It features a high turtleneck collar that snugly covers the throat and long, slim-fit straight sleeves that cleanly hug the arms, structuring a close-to-body silhouette. On the lower body, straight-leg lime-green denim trousers offer a structured, relaxed fit, cropping slightly above the ankles, while the head remains completely bare. Completing the look is a pair of rugged white leather combat boots with thick, lugged platform soles and functional white laces.
