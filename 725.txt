This casual outfit features an oversized white sweatshirt paired with light-wash blue jeans and chunky white platform boots. The heavy fleece sweatshirt dominates the upper half with its slouchy, drop-shoulder silhouette, voluminous sleeves, and a ribbed crewneck. It is partially tucked into high-waisted, straight-leg denim jeans that have a structured, rigid drape, a classic five-pocket design, belt loops, and folded cuffs at the ankle. Complete body coverage is maintained with no skin exposed. Heavy white combat boots with a highly exaggerated, deeply treaded platform sole anchor the look, adding stark geometric contrast to the relaxed denim and knitwear.
